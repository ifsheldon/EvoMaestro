<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces a native Python Adam Optimizer that runs immediately after the grid search. The grid search is excellent at finding a robust global topological initialization (e.g., deciding which side of the school to pass), but it is limited by its rigid Gaussian basis shapes. By analytically differentiating the surrogate objectives (`F1`, `F2`, and `F3`) with respect to every `Y` coordinate, we can treat the path as a completely flexible discrete spline (a rubber-band in an energy field). 

The `F1` gradient naturally behaves like a Laplacian smoothing operator, while `F2` repels the path from the school and `F3` attracts it to the base station. Adam fine-tunes the coordinates for 150 steps, perfectly balancing the competing objectives and significantly lowering the final score.
</DESCRIPTION>
