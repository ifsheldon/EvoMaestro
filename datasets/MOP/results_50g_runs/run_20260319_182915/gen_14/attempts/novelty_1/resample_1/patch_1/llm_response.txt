<DESCRIPTION>
The current heuristic path optimization is limited by three factors: 
1) The clipping of the path offset (max_offset=22.0) prevents the drone from reaching the base station's Y-coordinate (approx -30), leading to high signal loss (F3).
2) The school repulsion and base attraction weights are too conservative.
3) The sinusoidal envelope is too narrow at the points of interest (x=40 and x=70).

This edit improves the path by:
- Increasing the max_offset to 60.0 to allow full proximity to the base station.
- Using a power-transformed sine envelope (sin^0.7) to create a flatter "plateau" in the middle of the path, allowing the drone to stay near the base station for a longer duration.
- Implementing dynamic repulsion that scales with the distance between the baseline and the school, ensuring a more consistent clearance.
- Increasing the influence (sigma) of both the school and base station to create smoother, earlier transitions that minimize the product of F1, F2, and F3.
