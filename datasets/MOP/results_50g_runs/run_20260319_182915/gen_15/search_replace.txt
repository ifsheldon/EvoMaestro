<DESCRIPTION>
Introduces a second-order curvature penalty (bi-Laplacian) to the active contour model. This allows the path to bend smoothly around the school and towards the base station without incurring the high distance (F1) penalties associated with jagged, localized deformations. The learning rate is slightly reduced and iterations increased to ensure stable convergence with the new higher-order gradients.
