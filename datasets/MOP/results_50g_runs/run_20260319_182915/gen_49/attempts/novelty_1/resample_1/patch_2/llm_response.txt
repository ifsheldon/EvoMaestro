<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements a two-scale Gaussian basis for the drone path generation and updates the surrogate objective to use a product-based score (F1 * F2 * F3), which more accurately reflects the MOP Multiplier Score used in the evaluation. 

1.  **CurveGenerator**: Expanded to include 6 additional broad Gaussians spread across the path span. This provides better global shaping to minimize signal loss (F3) while maintaining the ability to avoid school noise (F2).
2.  **SurrogateEvaluator**: Switched from a weighted sum to a product-based objective `(f1 * w1) * (f2 * w2) * (f3 * w3)` with a smoothness regularizer. This aligns the internal optimization with the external grading metric.
3.  **LocalSearchOptimizer**: Updated to handle the expanded 12-parameter space (6 original + 6 broad amplitudes). The coordinate-wise descent now optimizes all 12 parameters, and seeds are expanded to initialize the new broad components.
</DESCRIPTION>
