<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements a two-scale Gaussian basis for path generation and switches the surrogate objective to a product-based metric (F1 * F2 * F3) to align with the grader's Multiplier Score.
1.  **CurveGenerator**: Expanded to include 4 additional broad Gaussian basis functions (sigma ~ 0.4 * span) for better global shaping of the path, particularly to minimize signal loss (F3).
2.  **SurrogateEvaluator**: The `score` function now calculates the product of the three objectives, which more accurately reflects the combined score used in the evaluation. A small smoothness penalty is retained to ensure path stability.
3.  **LocalSearchOptimizer**: Updated to handle the new 10-dimensional parameter space (6 original + 4 broad amplitudes). Seeds and step sizes are adjusted to accommodate the expanded parameterization.
</DESCRIPTION>
