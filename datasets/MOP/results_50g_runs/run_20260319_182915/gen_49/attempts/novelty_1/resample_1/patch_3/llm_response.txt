<DESCRIPTION>
Implement a two-scale Gaussian basis (6 local + 6 broad basis functions) and switch the internal surrogate objective from a weighted sum to a product-based metric (F1*F2*F3) to match the external grader's Multiplier Score. The local search optimization is extended from 6 to 12 dimensions to handle the broad amplitudes.
