<DESCRIPTION>
To minimize the combined score (F1 * F2 * F3), we replace the straight-line path with a sinusoidal arch. This arch is designed to bend away from the school located at (40, 20) and pass extremely close to the base station at (70, -30). Since the school is at a positive Y-coordinate and the base station is at a negative Y-coordinate, a downward arch simultaneously decreases the school noise proximity (F2) and the base station signal drop (F3). An amplitude of 37.1 is used to ensure the path passes almost exactly through y = -30 when x = 70 (progress = 0.7), which significantly reduces F3 while maintaining a reasonable path length (F1).
