<DESCRIPTION>
This modification implements the recommended two-stage adaptive school repulsion. It first projects the drone's path based on the base station's attraction and tail bias, then calculates the clearance from the school at its x-coordinate. A sharp school push is then applied with an amplitude that adaptively increases if the clearance is small, following the suggested formula `amp = min(52.0, max(32.0, a + b / (clearance + c)))`. Additionally, the surrogate score weights have been rebalanced to prioritize minimizing F3 (Signal Loss) and F2 (School Noise).
