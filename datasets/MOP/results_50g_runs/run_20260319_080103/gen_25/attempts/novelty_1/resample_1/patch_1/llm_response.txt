To minimize the product score simultaneously:
1. We switch carefully from a naive sine-wave baseline offset to localized Gaussian offsets centered on the specific objects (`sx` and `bx`), resolving path-dragging issues and making the route efficiently straight in non-critical segments.
2. The width constants (`sigma_school=12.0`, `sigma_base=18.0`) ensure we dip exactly where needed without ruining path straightness.
3. Crucially (as suggested), we actively condition the base-attraction Gaussian to weaken where the school-repulsion Gaussian is strongest using an overlap penalty `w_b * (1.0 - 0.8 * w_s)`. This avoids counter-productive target conflict when crossing the X-area near the school while restoring optimal Base-Station attraction globally.
4. Keeping the `edge_taper = sin(pi * pct)` effectively connects these dips smoothly back onto the straight-line origin/destination points.
