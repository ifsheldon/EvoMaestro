An excellent way to improve the score is to target the dominant weakness, which is `F3` (Signal Loss). Currently, the attraction to the base station is too weak and too broad, resulting in insufficient signal improvement while still adding to the flying distance.

We can strengthen the base station's pull (increase the amplitude multiplier to `0.80`) while simultaneously narrowing its area of effect (`sigma_base` reduced from `0.18` to `0.14`). This creates a more decisive, localized curve towards the base station without bending the entire path, significantly improving `F3` while keeping `F1` (Distance) and `F2` (School Noise) low. We also need to increase the `max_dev` clamp to allow this stronger localized pull.
