<DESCRIPTION>
The previous heuristic applied two independent Gaussians (one repelling from the school, one attracting to the base). Due to superposition, the exact safe offsets at `(sx, sy)` and `(bx, by)` were distorted by the tails of the other Gaussian, sometimes creating a "double-dip" that needlessly increased the curve length (worsening F1) or missing the target depth (worsening F2/F3).

To guarantee optimal positioning at exactly `x = sx` (safely away from the school) and `x = bx` (right at the base) while maintaining single-valley mathematical smoothness to drastically improve F1, we introduce an exact 2-Point Gaussian Interpolator. By establishing the precise target coordinates for the school-avoidance peak and the base-attraction valley, we can analytically solve the 2x2 linear system for the optimal Gaussian amplitudes `A_s` and `A_b`. This guarantees the final superposed curve intersects exactly the strategic waypoints while minimizing excess distance. It effectively shapes the path as a smooth envelope curving precisely away from (40, 20) and wrapping gently into (70, -30).
</DESCRIPTION>
