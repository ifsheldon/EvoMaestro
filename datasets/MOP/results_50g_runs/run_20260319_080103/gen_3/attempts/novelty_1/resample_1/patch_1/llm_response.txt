<DESCRIPTION>
This algorithm implements a dual-influence model using Gaussian kernels modulated by a sinusoidal envelope. 

Key insights:
1. **Multi-Objective Balance**: The three objectives (Distance, Noise, Signal) are balanced by treating the school as a repulsive force and the base station as an attractive force.
2. **Gaussian Influence**: Two Gaussian kernels are centered at the X-coordinates of the school and base station. The school kernel pushes the path vertically away from the school's Y-coordinate to minimize noise (F2), while the base station kernel pulls the path toward its Y-coordinate to maximize signal strength (F3).
3. **Sinusoidal Envelope**: To maintain the fixed start and end points (ensuring the path remains valid), the combined Gaussian offsets are multiplied by a sine-based envelope `sin(pi * t)`. This ensures the deviation is zero at the boundaries and smooth throughout the trajectory.
4. **Robustness**: The repulsion direction is determined by the path's general position relative to the school, preventing "kinks" and ensuring a smooth, differentiable curve that minimizes the distance penalty (F1).
