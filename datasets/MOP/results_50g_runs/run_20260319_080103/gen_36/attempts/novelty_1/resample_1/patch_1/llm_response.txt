Specifically, it narrows the school kernel (`sig_school`) to provide sharp local avoidance and broadens the base station kernel (`sig_base`) to provide a more consistent pull towards the signal source. Additionally, it rebalances the energy function weights by increasing the base station attraction (`w_base`) and school repulsion (`w_school`) while reducing the path length penalty (`w_len`) and smoothing constraint (`w_smooth`). This allows the path to bend more effectively towards the base station (reducing F3) while maintaining safety near the school (reducing F2). The number of iterations and the learning rate decay are also adjusted to ensure better convergence.
