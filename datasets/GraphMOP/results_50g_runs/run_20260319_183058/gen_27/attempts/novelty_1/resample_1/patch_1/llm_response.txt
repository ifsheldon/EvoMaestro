An excellent approach to lower latencies significantly and break out of locally suboptimal traps is to concentrate our optimization efforts on heavily loaded servers. Since end-to-end latency grows very fast (asymptotically) with the server utilization due to queueing delays, local refinements should be aggressively focused on moving tasks away from the most heavily utilized ("hot") servers rather than trying a broad and uniform reassignment pass across the entire graph. We can identify the top utilized servers and do a restricted repair pass multiple times.

<NAME>
restricted_server_focused_repair
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replaces the uniform one-pass local improvement loop with a multi-pass restricted local search. By sorting the servers by utilization and only considering tasks assigned to the top 3 "hot" servers for reassignment, the algorithm aggressively tackles the structural bottlenecks of high queueing delay (and its corresponding dynamic energy penalties limit).
</DESCRIPTION>
